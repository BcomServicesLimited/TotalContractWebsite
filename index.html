About Total Contract Services

Total Contract Services Pty Ltd is a major painting contractor specialising in large commercial and construction projects with a separate division specialising in long term maintenance. Apart from numerous Apartment and Commercial Projects.

TCS have also completed in excess of $50 million dollars worth of painting works over the past 25 years for Westfield Design and Construction now Centre D&C. Total Contract Services Pty Ltd has been operating in both NSW and QLD since 1992.

Our objective is to provide excellent service and to provide the very best possible quality in these projects to the satisfaction of the client. To ensure these objectives are met, the company has established a quality program to provide clients with the assurance that the services provided by TCS Pty Ltd will be consistently of good quality & reliability.
