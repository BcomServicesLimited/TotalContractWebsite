Who We Are

Queensland's Facilities Management Specialists

Total Contract Services is a leading facilities management company headquartered on the Gold Coast. We deliver end-to-end building maintenance, painting, and property services to some of South-East Queensland's most iconic commercial, retail, hospitality, and residential properties.

Our team of qualified tradespeople and project managers bring decades of combined experience, ensuring every property under our care is maintained to the highest standard — on time and on budget.

What We Do

Our Services

Comprehensive facilities management solutions tailored to your property's needs.

Facilities Management

Comprehensive management of your property's facilities including preventive maintenance scheduling, contractor coordination, and compliance management.

Painting & Decorating

Interior and exterior painting services including rope-access painting for high-rise buildings, surface preparation, and protective coatings.

Building Maintenance

Reactive and planned preventive maintenance across all building trades — electrical, plumbing, carpentry, and general repairs.

Strata & Body Corporate

Dedicated maintenance programs for strata-titled residential and commercial buildings, working closely with body corporate managers.

Safety & Compliance

WHS compliance inspections, fire safety maintenance, and regulatory reporting to keep your property safe and legally compliant.

Project Management

End-to-end project management for refurbishments, capital works, and construction projects — from scoping through to handover.
